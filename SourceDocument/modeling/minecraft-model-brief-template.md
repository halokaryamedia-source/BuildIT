## Acceptance Criteria

- Asset type and target platform are explicit.
- Scale target is defined relative to Minecraft blocks or player height.
- Required parts are listed before modelling begins.
- Cube/element budget is stated or marked `Assumption`.
- Atlas size, pixel style, and UV expectations are stated.
- Bone, pivot, and animation needs are stated or marked `Out of scope`.
- References are listed or marked `Needs verification`.
