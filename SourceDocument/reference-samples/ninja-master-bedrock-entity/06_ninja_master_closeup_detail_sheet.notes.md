# 06 Ninja Master Close-up Detail Sheet Notes

## Sheet Identity

- Image file: `06_ninja_master_closeup_detail_sheet.png`
- Sheet purpose: visual callout guide for small details that are easy to misread during modelling and texturing.
- Current modelling phase supported: Geometry Detailing / Base Texturing / Detail Texturing.
- Priority: High.
- Status: Approved.

## What Codex Should Read From This Sheet

- Use this sheet after Sheet 04 and Sheet 05.
- The image is intentionally light on text; use this notes file for the modelling and texturing interpretation.
- Sheet 04 defines major part groups.
- Sheet 05 defines color and texture placement.
- Sheet 06 clarifies close-up detail interpretation so small visual details are not accidentally modelled as extra cubes.

## Close-up Targets

- Head / Face / Beard: keep face pixels readable; beard and hair shading are mostly texture detail over the head mass.
- Shoulder / Armor Mass: keep the broad shoulder silhouette readable; gray armor must stay visually separate from teal robe.
- Belt / Straps: red is a narrow waist accent and motion cue; do not enlarge it into a major body part.
- Lower Robe / Cyan Strips: teal robe is the main lower mass; cyan strips are side accents, not skin or eye color.
- Shoes / Ground Contact: feet must touch the baseline and stay blocky.
- Top / 3/4 Detail Check: use these views only to confirm layering, depth, and overlap readability.
- Front / Side / Back Check: use the bottom views to confirm that close-up decisions still match the full-body silhouette from the three primary directions.

## Geometry vs Texture

Build as geometry:

- large head mass
- shoulders / armor mass
- torso and robe panels
- belt volume where it affects silhouette
- shoes and feet

Keep as texture-only:

- beard shading
- eye pixels
- robe checker pattern
- metal highlights
- tiny trim pixels
- small color seams

## Do Not Misinterpret

- Do not turn pixel clusters into cubes.
- Do not add tiny geometry for robe checker shading.
- Do not treat cyan robe side strips as face, skin, or eye color.
- Do not let the feet float above the baseline.
- Do not use top or 3/4 view as the main dimension source.

## Pass / Fail Use

Pass if:

- close-up details remain readable from front, side, back, top, and 3/4.
- geometry additions are limited to parts that affect silhouette.
- texture-only details remain texture-only.
- Sheet 04 part groups and Sheet 05 color roles remain consistent.

Fail if:

- small texture pixels become extra cubes.
- belt, beard, eye, or robe checker details become oversized.
- cyan robe strips are confused with face or eye colors.
- feet no longer touch the baseline.
