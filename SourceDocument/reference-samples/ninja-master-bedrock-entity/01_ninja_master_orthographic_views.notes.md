## What Codex Should Read From This Image

Use only the final sheet assembled from real Blockbench MCP screenshots. Do not use rejected projection images.

- The model is a tall humanoid / boss-like Bedrock Entity, not a player-height character.
- Scale values are locked in Sheet 02.
- Front view is the primary identity view.
- Side view should show shallow depth compared to height.
- Back view should preserve robe/cloth and back silhouette, without inventing new forms.
- Top view is footprint/layout only.
- 3/4 view is preview only, not a measurement source.

- upper torso / neck transition

- hair/top knot pieces
- beard pieces
- left and right upper arms
- left and right lower arms
- belt / front buckle
- hanging belt straps
- side cloth panels
- front and rear cloth panels

- lower legs

## Texture-Only Details

Treat these as texture-only:

- fabric folds
- small seam lines
- cloth edge shading
- face pixels
- hair texture
- belt markings
- tiny costume trim
- subtle material gradients

## Do Not Misinterpret

- Do not make the model player-height.
- Do not smooth the humanoid into fantasy concept art.
- Do not turn cloth folds or costume trim into excessive small cubes.
- Do not use top or 3/4 view as exact dimensions.
- Do not change the tall boss-like silhouette.

## Pass / Fail Use

Pass if the sheet preserves:

- tall humanoid silhouette
- broad head/hood read
- visible torso, waist, legs, arms, feet, belt, and cloth panel separation
- shallow side depth
- blocky Minecraft/Blockbench construction

Fail if:

- height reads close to player height
- side view becomes too deep or bulky
- cloth detail becomes noisy geometry
- proportions drift away from the Sheet 02 scale values
