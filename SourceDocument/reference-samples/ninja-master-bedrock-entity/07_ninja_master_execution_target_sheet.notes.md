## What Codex Should Read From This Sheet

## DO Targets

- Lock the 4 main views: front, side, back, and top are the main visual authority before detailing.
- Build to the silhouette / mass read shown on the sheet.
- Keep small details as texture where the sheet shows face, beard, eyes, belt, and straps.
- Final check before polish: re-check front, side, back, top, and 3/4; feet must touch baseline and proportions must match the reference.

## Non-Visual Rules Kept In Notes

- Build in this order: silhouette, major cuboid masses, part layering, UV/base texture, then small texture detail.
- Keep material roles locked: teal robe is main, white head/beard is focal, red belt and cyan side strips are accents only.
- Follow phase gates from the main workflow; do not use this image as a phase-gate replacement.

## Execution Order

1. Primary silhouette.
2. Major cuboid masses.
3. Part layering.
4. UV / base texture.
5. Small texture detail.
6. Final view check.

## Pass / Fail Use

Pass if:

- the model still reads like the target from front, side, back, top, and 3/4.
- detail work never damages the major silhouette.
- texture-only details stay texture-only.
- Sheet 04 part grouping and Sheet 05 material hierarchy remain consistent.

Fail if:

- small detail work changes the core proportions.
- red or cyan accents overpower the teal robe.
- beard, eyes, checker pixels, or trims become unnecessary cubes.
- feet lose baseline contact.
