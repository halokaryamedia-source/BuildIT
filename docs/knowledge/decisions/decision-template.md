# Decision Template

Copy this when recording a new decision.

```md
# <decision title>

## Context

What problem or change triggered the decision?

## Decision

What did we choose?

## Why

Why is this the right choice for this repo?

## Tradeoffs

What did we gain and what did we give up?

## Validation

What proof do we have, or what still needs validation?

## Follow-up

What should happen next, if anything?
```
