# Pixel Art Silhouette

## Purpose

Make the subject readable before color, shading, or surface detail.

## Silhouette checks

Evaluate as applicable:

```text
primary axis
width / height relationship
major protrusions
negative-space landmarks
functional openings
asymmetry
visual center
```

## Simplification

At small grids, simplify continuous contours into the fewest stepped forms that preserve identity.

Prefer a clean characteristic contour over literal tracing of every source-image bump or curve.

## Negative space

Negative space can be as important as the outer contour. Preserve holes, hooks, handles, gaps, and separations that define the object.

## Functional exaggeration

Selective exaggeration is allowed when required for recognition at target size. Enlarge the identity-critical feature, not the entire object indiscriminately.

## Failure ownership

If the asset cannot be recognized without its internal shading, repair silhouette/composition first. Do not attempt to solve a silhouette failure with extra colors, outlines, or texture noise.

## Native-size validation

Always judge silhouette at actual target scale, not only in an enlarged editor view.
