# Blockbench Geometry

Use only with `BEDROCK_CUBOID_GEOMETRY` or `GEOMETRY_LOCAL_REPAIR`. Read Production Context, Reference Visual, `GEOMETRY.md`, and state. Build Primary Form then Structural Detail, preserve accepted areas, checkpoint, capture five standard views, validate, and stop at `GEOMETRY_REVIEW`.

No texture, UV, mesh, Animation, export, or unrequired geometry work.
